## **Hi, I'm Corbett McKinney.**
I’m a writer living in Asheville, NC, with a background in national and federal service and nonprofit work. I write speculative stories and essays that channel the hope and anxiety I feel for the future into
something positive. I'm currently querying my first novel-length project. You can join my newsletter to follow along on my journey!
